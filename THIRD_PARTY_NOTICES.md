# Attribution and third-party terms

OIPA-Net uses the public GSV-Cities vision–language protocols and shares benchmark utilities with the [original retrieval release](https://github.com/CV4RA/AcrossPlace/tree/2a66c25f3a241c2af93eae9dbde658f3e19cde67). It also includes protocols derived from [Mapillary Street-Level Sequences](https://www.mapillary.com/dataset/places) and [SF-XL](https://github.com/gmberton/CosPlace). Credits and applicable upstream terms follow each collection. The [GSV-Cities](https://github.com/amaralibey/gsv-cities), MSLS, and SF-XL image providers retain their respective rights. The software MIT license applies to repository code. The release bundles contain metadata and experiment records. Street-view photographs are acquired separately from the source releases.

Pretrained checkpoints are downloaded separately from [Meta](https://huggingface.co/facebook/PE-Core-L14-336), [Google](https://huggingface.co/google/siglip2-base-patch16-384), [Salesforce](https://huggingface.co/Salesforce/blip-itm-base-coco), and [OpenAI](https://huggingface.co/openai/clip-vit-base-patch16). The upstream licenses and model cards govern their weights and use. PyTorch, Transformers, PEFT, and other packages keep their respective terms. Historical protocol identifiers are kept in the frozen manifests for compatibility.
